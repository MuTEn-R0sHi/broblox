## BroBlox: The Platform Hub

**Concept:**
BroBlox is the central hub game for the entire BroBlox platform—a vibrant, solar-system-inspired world where each planet is a massive block. Players can walk and jump on all sides of these block-planets, thanks to a unique gravity system. Each face of a planet features a different biome and game type, with portals connecting to all other BroBlox games of that genre.

**LittleBro Integration:**
Your LittleBro companion (see IDEAS.md for mascot details) is at the heart of the BroBlox experience. Every player is accompanied by their customizable LittleBro, who reacts to gameplay, can be upgraded, and is visible across all BroBlox games. LittleBros live on the planet-blocks, and collecting, customizing, and showing off your LittleBros is a core part of the hub.

**Key Features:**

- **Gravity Block Worlds:** Explore, jump, and walk on all sides of giant block-planets, each with unique environments and challenges.
- **Biomes & Game Portals:** Each block face is a different biome and hosts a portal to a specific BroBlox game or minigame of that type.
- **LittleBro Progression:** Enhance your LittleBro with cosmetics, emotes, and meta-upgrades using the shared BroCoin currency. Unlock new hats, faces, and accessories by playing games and completing quests.
- **Collection & Quests:** Collect different LittleBros, complete story-driven quests, and discover rare variants.
- **Player Home Area:** Every player has a customizable home space (zoo, trophy room, treasure cave) to display their best LittleBros, pets, and achievements from all BroBlox games.
- **BroLympics Event:** Regular platform-wide events where players compete in multiple disciplines (minigames) for rewards and recognition.
- **Cross-Game Hub:** The BroBlox hub is the main entry point to all current and future BroBlox games, enhancing brand recognition and player retention.

**Vision:**
BroBlox is designed to be the social and creative heart of the platform, encouraging players to collect, customize, and show off their LittleBros while seamlessly discovering and joining new games. The hub strengthens the BroBlox brand and provides a persistent, rewarding experience that ties all games together.
