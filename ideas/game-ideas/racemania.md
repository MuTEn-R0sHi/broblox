## Race Mania 🏇 (Fairground Racing Game)

**Genre:** Competitive Arcade / Pet Racing  
**Inspiration:** Real-life fairground horse/camel racing games (ball rolling, scoring holes)

### Core Concept

Players roll balls into scoring holes to advance their racing pet along a track. The higher the score, the further and faster their pet moves. All pets race side-by-side, just like the classic carnival game.

### Gameplay Loop

1. **Select Pet:** Choose from your collection of world-appropriate racing pets (horse, camel, snail, etc.).
2. **Race:** Roll balls to score points and move your pet forward. Compete against others in real time.
3. **Win & Progress:** Finish tracks to earn wins, unlock new worlds, and collect rare pets.
4. **Upgrade:** Train pets and equip items to boost speed, add effects, or unlock special abilities.
5. **Unlock Worlds:** Progress through increasingly challenging worlds, each with a unique racing animal (e.g., horses are fastest, snails are slowest).

### Features

- **Absurdly High Numbers:** Progress, scores, and pet stats can reach ridiculous values (K, M, B, T, Qa, Qi, Sx, Sp, Oc, No, Dc, etc.), similar to popular idle/clicker games. This creates a sense of endless escalation and spectacle.
- **Multiplayer Races:** Up to 8 players per track, all visible in real time.
- **Pet Collection:** Hatch, buy, and train pets. Each world features unique animals.
- **World Progression:** Unlock new tracks and animals (horse, camel, ostrich, turtle, snail, etc.).
- **Pet Upgrades:** Train stats, equip items, and unlock visual effects for high-end pets.
- **Skill & Luck:** Scoring is a mix of skill (aiming) and luck (ball rolls).
- **Spectacle:** High-level races feature wild effects, super-fast pets, and dramatic finishes.

### Unique Selling Points

1. **Classic Arcade Feel:** Nostalgic gameplay with modern multiplayer twist.
2. **Collection & Customization:** Deep pet system with upgrades and cosmetics.
3. **Progression:** Unlock slower, more challenging worlds for bragging rights.
4. **Social Play:** Great for parties, events, and streaming.
5. **Absurd Numbers:** The thrill of seeing your stats and scores reach astronomical values.

### Extra Ideas & Inspiration

- **Seasonal Events:** Limited-time animals (reindeer, penguin, etc.) and themed tracks.
- **Leaderboards:** Fastest times, most wins, rarest pets, highest score ever (with absurd numbers).
- **Mini-games:** Training challenges, pet races outside the main track.
- **Spectator Mode:** Watch high-stakes races with wild effects.
- **Ticket Redemption:** Like real carnival games, players can earn tickets for wins and spend them on exclusive pets or cosmetics.
