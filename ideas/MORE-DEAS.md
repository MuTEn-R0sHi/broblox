## game map types & generator system

each type has its parameters/seeds to configure generation and comes with all the required base setup like: play area boundaries, specific theming and environmental additions. for the world plane we want to generate realistic maps with: endless horizon simulation, naturalistic elevations like hills and mountains and river beds and canyons etc., water system with rivers and shorelines and islands and oceans. as well as a system to create logical streets/roads and village/city layouts, all realistic and configurable and themeable. also all effects like light, fog, weather, etc. should work together nicely.
