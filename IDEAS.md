### Adapt common trending roblox game features

## Rarity Hierarchy

Common (Gray/White): Easiest to find, low value.
Uncommon (Green): Slightly harder to find.
Rare (Blue): Standard for mid-game items.
Epic (Purple): Difficult to obtain.
Legendary (Red/Gold): Very hard to obtain, high stats.
Mythical/Mythic (Orange/Pink): Extremely rare.
Celestial/Divine/Secret (Various): Often 1 in 1 million or higher chances.

## Roblox Moments (In-Game Recording)

A major new feature allowing users to record, edit, and share their best, funniest, or most intense moments directly from the app (similar to TikTok).
